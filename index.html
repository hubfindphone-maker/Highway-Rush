How to Play

1. Press START RACE.

2. Move left and right to avoid traffic.

3. Your score increases while driving.

4. Faster cars give faster scoring.

5. Every 500 score points changes the season.

6. After Winter, Spring starts again.

7. Every 2,500 score points increases the Stage.

8. The multiplier increases every new Stage.

9. Use cash to unlock faster cars.

📱 Mobile: Left / Right buttons.
